Hi, I'm Uurtsaikh.

I'm a physician, public health researcher, and product developer based in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. My work connects epidemiology, statistical analysis, and practical tools to support health research and public policy.

I am Chief Product Developer at **Axiom Inc.** and a Project Manager and Researcher at the **Onom Foundation and Liver Center**. My research interests include hypertension and cardiovascular health, liver disease, environmental exposures, and the use of population health data to understand gaps in prevention and care.

Previously, I worked as a **Senior Researcher and Statistician** at Mongolia's Center for Health Development and as a **Researcher** at the Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation (IHME), University of Washington. At IHME, I worked on liver disease and cirrhosis within the Global Burden of Disease study.

I earned my **Master of Public Health in Global Health** from the University of Washington in 2024 with support from the Fulbright Student Program, and my **Medical Doctor degree in Traditional Medicine** from the Mongolian National University of Medical Sciences in 2015.

## Research

My work focuses on:

- **Cardiometabolic health:** hypertension, diabetes, risk factors, and gaps in awareness, treatment, and control.
- **Environmental health:** air pollution, spatial epidemiology, and cardiovascular health.
- **Liver disease and infectious diseases:** viral hepatitis, cirrhosis, and population health research.
- **Nutrition and health equity:** acculturation, dietary sodium-to-potassium ratios, and differences across population groups.
- **Methods:** complex survey analysis, longitudinal and trend analysis, GIS, data visualization, and applications of machine learning.

[Explore my research and projects](/research/).

## Selected research experience

- Led field research and statistical analysis for a population-based hypertension survey involving **4,515 adults in Ulaanbaatar**.
- Contributed to studies of hypertension knowledge, attitudes, and practices among primary care professionals.
- Led disease burden estimation work to support health policy at the Center for Health Development.
- Co-authored research on diabetes care, hypertension, and antibody responses to COVID-19 vaccines.
